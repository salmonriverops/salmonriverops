**Forward-deployed engineer and operator. I run and grow service businesses: the people, the demand, the operation, and the systems underneath.**

I am the General Manager of [Adventure Idaho](https://www.adventureidahorafting.com/), a multi-site outdoor recreation company. I have worked across product, demand, pricing, hiring, training, field operations, and the systems that connect them.

The company grew 3.6x while we raised prices, maintained a 5.0 rating across 650 Google reviews, and expanded to 44 seasonal staff across two bases. I then designed and built the systems underneath the operation, the data model, the automations and connectors, and the apps the crew uses, so that growth no longer had to run through one person's memory.

[See the full work and case studies](https://justinsmith.build/)

## What I build

- **Operating systems for real businesses.** Connected models of customers, work, people, vehicles, equipment, qualifications, capacity, money, and exceptions.
- **Systems of action, not passive dashboards.** The system surfaces conflicts, missing information, capacity limits, and decisions that require a person.
- **Human-in-the-loop AI.** Software and AI can read, check, and propose. Decisions involving money, safety, compliance, employees, or customer relationships still stop for a person.
- **The right tool, not always new software.** Some problems need AI, some need a connection between tools the business already pays for, some need a hard rule, and some need to be left with a good person. Deciding which is most of the work.
- **Tools built with the people who use them.** I work inside the operation, learn the rules that are not written down, run the new system alongside the old one, and revise it until the team trusts it.

## Selected work

### Adventure Idaho operating platform

A live operating model connecting bookings, staffing, qualifications, vehicles, equipment, food, capacity, maintenance, collections, and daily changes. Weekly planning fell from 20 to 40 hours to roughly 2 to 4.

[Operating platform](https://justinsmith.build/work/operating-platform/) · [Dispatch board](https://justinsmith.build/work/dispatch/) · [Communications layer](https://justinsmith.build/work/communications-layer/)

### Constraint discovery battery

A deterministic, human-in-the-loop tool that reads operational exports and proposes candidate business constraints for an operator to confirm or reject. It runs locally with Python and DuckDB, and real business data never enters Git.

[View the repository](https://github.com/salmonriverops/constraint-battery)

### Personal OS

A private system for my own work and life. One press of a shortcut button on my phone captures a voice note, and it gets transcribed and routed to the right place: a task, a list, a note, a log. The point is removing friction: if capturing something takes more than a few seconds, it does not get captured. It is the same problem as the operation at a smaller scale, turning scattered context into a system that stays useful without handing consequential decisions to automation.
